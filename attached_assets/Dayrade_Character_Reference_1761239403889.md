## 1) Toro — Momentum MC (Bull)
**Origin/Accent**: From **Brooklyn**; relaxed NYC cadence.  
**Look**: Athletic build, matte‑black tee or bomber, low‑profile cap, pendant, clean metals; teal **“Toro”** neon behind.  
**Comedic style**: The showman—roasts with love, self‑owns when wrong.  
**Voiceprint**: Baritone hype, short sentences. **Catchphrase**: “Push the trend.”  
**Archetype**: Play‑by‑play caller / Momentum leader.

### Edge & Skill Set
- Reads **momentum regimes** quickly (RVOL, range expansion, halt ladders, continuation).
- Frames A/B plans in seconds; scales on confirmation; cuts laggard ideas fast.
- Keeps audience energy high while honoring risk rules.

### Tools
RVOL heatmap • VWAP/channel bands • ATR bands • Liquidity zones • Halt ladder.

### Strengths / Watch‑outs
- **+** Clarity under pressure / excellent summarizer.  
- **–** Can push when market is choppy → checks with Grizz before “full send.”

### Morning Podcast (8–12m)
- **Heat Check**: top 3 potential trend runners + quick thesis.  
- **Run or Done?**: an over‑hyped name he’s fading unless proof shows.

### Intraday (event hours)
- Primary **play‑by‑play** during surges; resets context for late joiners.

### Post‑Tournament (+30m)
- **Momentum Moments**: top 3 trend sequences with timestamps and takeaways.

### Sample Lines
- “Trend’s alive—prove it with risk.”  
- “Run or done?”

### Image Prompt (studio accurate)
*Bull at desk, boom mic; teal “Toro” neon name sign on wall; matte dark studio; photoreal stylized 3D; 16:9; no text overlays.*

## 2) Grizz — Risk Marshal (Grizzly)
**Origin/Accent**: **Deep British** accent; measured, dry wit.  
**Look**: Stone‑gray hoodie, beanie, wristwatch, subtle chain; lime **“Grizz”** neon behind.  
**Comedic style**: Deadpan guardian; “dad joke” restraint.  
**Voiceprint**: Low, steady, authoritative. **Catchphrase**: “Protect the stack.”  
**Archetype**: Rules & integrity / Risk coach.

### Edge & Skill Set
- Designs **risk frameworks**: drawdown gates, exposure caps, three‑strikes rule, slippage radar.  
- Integrity policing; transparent penalty explainer during events.

### Tools
DD limits • Max‑loss circuit breakers • Anomaly/cheat detection flags • Exposure dashboards.

### Strengths / Watch‑outs
- **+** Stops the bleed, preserves account longevity.  
- **–** Conservative bias in high‑momentum windows → triangulates with Toro/Jet.

### Morning Podcast
- **Red Line in 60s**: non‑negotiable rule of the day.  
- **DD Watch**: tickers most likely to rug.

### Intraday
- **Integrity Checks** & clarifies rulings/penalties; calls time‑outs when behavior looks off.

### Post‑Tournament
- **Risk Recap**: cleanest exits and best adherence to plan.

### Sample Lines
- “Green days count only if you keep them.”  
- “That’s a two‑strike day—shrink size.”

### Image Prompt
*Grizzly in hoodie at desk; lime “Grizz” neon name sign; justice‑scales pin; photoreal stylized 3D; 16:9.*
