1. Overview of Dayrade®

Dayrade® is a competitive trading tournament platform often described as an “eSport for day traders.” It merges the thrill of live competition with the fast-paced nature of financial markets, offering multiple divisions, cash prizes, sponsored trading accounts, and an engaging environment for both newcomers and experienced traders.

Key Highlights:

• Live-streamed trading competitions

• Interactive dashboards and real-time leaderboards

• Expert commentary during tournaments

• Open to participants worldwide (where legally permissible)

• Multiple sponsorship and advertising opportunities

2. Tournaments and Divisions

2.1 Elevator Division

• Type: Simulation Trading (SIM) Tournament

• Duration: Monthly, from the 1st through the end of each month

• Prize/Benefit:

◦ The top 10% of participants earn a free ticket (valued at $69) to the Crusader Tournament, where they can compete for $1,000 Zimtra trading account (no cash equivalent).

• Ideal For: Beginners and those looking to learn or practice trading in a risk-free environment.

2.2 Crusader Division

• Type: Simulation Trading (SIM) Tournament

• Duration: 3-day event (dates announced on the Dayrade® website)

• Prizes (10 winners):

◦ $1,000 Zimtra trading account (no cash equivalent)

• Ideal For: Intermediate-level traders honing their skills and competing for cash or a sponsored trading account.

2.3 Raider Division

• Type: Real-money professional trading tournament

• Duration: 5-day event (dates announced on the Dayrade® website)

• Prize Categories:

1 Most Profitable Trader

▪ $50,000 cash

▪ $250,000 Zimtra trading account with a $20,000 stop limit

▪ Keep all earnings generated in this account

2 Largest Volume of Shares (Over $1)

▪ $10,000 cash

▪ $50,000 Zimtra trading account with a $2,500 stop limit

▪ Keep all earnings generated in this account

3 Single Most Profitable Trade

▪ $10,000 cash

▪ $50,000 Zimtra trading account with a $2,500 stop limit

▪ Keep all earnings generated in this account

• Trading Rules (Raider Division):

◦ Leverage: 1:20

◦ Maximum Loss: $10,000

◦ Maximum Daily Loss: $5,000
