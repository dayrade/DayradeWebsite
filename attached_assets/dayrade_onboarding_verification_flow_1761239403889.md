# 1) Executive Summary (1–2 short paragraphs)
DAYRADE leads new sign-ups through a modal that collects name, email, and password, then pushes them directly into an eight-step onboarding wizard covering social handles, avatar, referrals, notification preferences, and terms so the profile meets participation requirements before users encounter gated features (`frontend_code/src/components/auth/AuthModal.tsx:31`, `frontend_code/src/components/auth/AuthModal.tsx:131`, `frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/OnboardingWizard.tsx:73`).  
Completion of KYC via Zimtra is the critical unlock—without it, users can explore but cannot access the trading account or buy tournament tickets; once Zimtra verification passes and a ticket is purchased, DAYRADE enables the simulator account, trading tools, and tournament entry (`Dayrade Platform - Single Source of Truth Documentation.md:176`, `frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/steps/StepDone.tsx:261`, `frontend_code/src/components/tournament/TournamentModal.tsx:304`).

# 2) End-to-End Journey Map (High Level)
1. **Sign Up / Create Account** — Users open the auth modal from the dashboard and submit name, email, and password to register or log in (`frontend_code/src/pages/Index.tsx:95`, `frontend_code/src/components/auth/AuthModal.tsx:31`).
2. **Welcome -> Onboarding Wizard** — Successful registration redirects to the eight-step onboarding experience for profile setup, avatar creation, referral tools, notifications, and terms acceptance (`frontend_code/src/components/auth/AuthModal.tsx:161`, `frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/OnboardingWizard.tsx:73`).
3. **Path Choice: Explore vs. Get Verified** — After onboarding, users can browse with limited permissions while DAYRADE highlights the need to verify to unlock more features (`frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/steps/StepWelcome.tsx:34`, `frontend_code/src/components/auth/UserStateIndicator.tsx:33`, `frontend_code/src/components/TopBar.tsx:526`).
4. **Verification (identity review)** — Starting KYC launches Zimtra’s flow with clear requirements, status tracking, and retry guidance if documents fail (`frontend_code/src/components/tournament/UserJourneyFlow.tsx:266`, `frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/steps/StepDone.tsx:261`, `frontend_code/src/components/kyc/KYCStatusTracker.tsx:296`).
5. **Post-Verification: Zimtra trading account access** — Approved users are flagged as fully verified, can proceed to Zimtra onboarding, and unlock simulator trading privileges once the tournament ticket is in place (`Dayrade Platform - Single Source of Truth Documentation.md:205`, `frontend_code/src/components/onboarding-guide/wizard/steps/StepDone.tsx:261`, `frontend_code/src/contexts/AuthContext.tsx:637`).
6. **Ticket Purchase Eligibility** — Verified users can launch the purchase flow; DAYRADE blocks unverified users with an alert and directs them back to verification (`Dayrade Platform - Single Source of Truth Documentation.md:184`, `frontend_code/src/components/tournament/TournamentModal.tsx:304`).
7. **Confirmation & Next Best Actions** — Ticket confirmation messaging steers players back to the dashboard and into countdown communications ahead of the event (`frontend_code/src/components/tournament/UserJourneyFlow.tsx:421`, `Dayrade Platform - Single Source of Truth Documentation.md:221`).

*Flow:* Sign Up -> Onboarding Wizard -> Explore (Limited) -> Verify -> Zimtra Access -> Ticket Purchase -> Confirmation
