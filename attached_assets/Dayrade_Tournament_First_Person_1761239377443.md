## What you’ll learn (and why it helps your real life)

### 1) Mindset & habits that stick
- **Pay yourself first → Risk budget first.** You’ll reserve “risk capital” before you trade — the same habit that powers real‑life saving.
- **Emergency fund logic → Never go all‑in.** Keep a buffer so one surprise doesn’t wreck your plan.
- **Budget discipline → Position sizing & daily loss limits.** Allocate thoughtfully. Cap the downside.
- **Consistency beats heroics.** Small, repeatable edges win tournaments — and build wealth.
- **Record‑keeping → Journaling with tags.** Track decisions, spot biases, improve faster.

### 2) Core market literacy
- **Instruments:** stocks, ETFs, indices; how commodities, currencies, and rates interact.
- **Micro vs. macro:** earnings and product cycles vs. inflation, jobs, central banks, geopolitics.
- **Liquidity & volatility:** when to size down or sit out.
- **Catalysts:** CPI, jobs reports, OPEC meetings, FOMC decisions, earnings calls.
- **How prices form:** order books, supply/demand, expectations.

### 3) Risk & decision quality
- **Position sizing methods:** fixed‑fractional, volatility‑adjusted, risk‑unit (R) models.
- **Stops & exits:** define invalidation first; act without emotion.
- **Expected value (EV):** win‑rate × payoff; one bad bet can undo many good ones.
- **Diversification:** manage correlations; avoid one‑factor concentration.
- **Behavioral bias control:** FOMO, loss aversion, anchoring, recency — notice and neutralize.

### 4) World knowledge → your wallet
You’ll practice causal chains so headlines become practical money insight:
- **Geopolitics → Oil → Transport → CPI → Your budget.**
- **Interest rates → Mortgages/loans → Housing/autos → Your savings rate.**
- **Tech cycles (AI) → Labor demand → Wages → Your reskilling ROI.**
- **Climate & insurance → Premiums → Your monthly cash flow.**

## Quick glossary
- **R (Risk unit):** Your preset loss amount per trade (e.g., 1% of account). Think in R to compare strategies.
- **EV (Expected Value):** Average outcome over many repeats; good decisions can still lose short‑term.
- **Drawdown:** Peak‑to‑trough decline; deeper = harder to recover.
- **Catalyst:** A news event that can move prices (scheduled or surprise).
- **Volatility:** Size/speed of moves; size down when volatility is high.

---

## Risk rules cheat sheet
- **Max risk per trade:** ~0.5–1.0 R (≈0.5–1% of account if using R).
- **Daily max drawdown:** ~2–3 R; stop for the day if hit.
- **One trade at a time** (as a beginner); quality over quantity.
- **Only A‑setups.** Grade ideas; skip B/C boredom trades.
- **Journal every trade within 24 hours.** If it isn’t documented, it didn’t happen.
