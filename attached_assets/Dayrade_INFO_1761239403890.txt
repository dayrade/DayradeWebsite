10) FAQ Draft (Copy-First)
Is DAYRADE gambling?

No. DAYRADE is a skill-based tournament platform with published rules and transparent scoring. We reward disciplined, consistent trading—not chance.
How are scores set?

Scoring rewards clean execution, risk control, and consistent performance. The rulebook uses simple definitions and tie-breakers you can read in two minutes.
Who can join?

Most retail traders can join, subject to regional eligibility and age requirements. Check the eligibility list during onboarding.
What does it cost?

Core features are free; optional upgrades and sponsor-backed perks may be offered (TBD). Pricing will always be clearly labeled.
What regions are supported?

Availability varies by region and local policy. The onboarding flow tells you instantly if you’re eligible.
What do I win?

Prizes are framed as buying-power rewards and status recognition. Prize ladders are published for each bracket.
Can I practice first?

Yes. Try sim mode with the same rules so you can build confidence before entering a live bracket.
How fair is this?

Very. We use anti-cheat monitoring, verified results, and clear rule enforcement. Integrity badges are visible across the arena.
Will rules change mid-tournament?

No. Rules won’t change during an active bracket. Any updates are announced ahead with effective dates.
How do divisions work?

Elevator is entry-level, Crusader is mid-tier, Raider is elite. Win and maintain form to climb and keep your spot.
Can spectators participate?

Absolutely. Watch highlights, follow traders, share placements, and join when you’re ready.
What if I miss check-in?

You’ll be prompted to re-enter in the next available window. Enable reminders to prevent misses.
How do sponsors fit in?

Sponsors support fair-play competition and provide perks or prize pools. Sponsor integrations never affect competitive outcomes.
Can I share my profile?

Yes. Use your Trader Card link or QR code to share on socials, streams, or at events.
How do I contact support?

Visit Help & FAQ and tap “Ask Support.” You’ll get a quick response with clear next steps.
